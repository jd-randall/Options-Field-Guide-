# Options Field Guide

An interactive, single-page guide to options. Open `options-field-guide.html` in a browser; no build step or server needed.

## What's inside

- **Rights and obligations**: long vs. short calls and puts, with a payoff/profit chart you can drag (defaults to the textbook $80-strike call costing $14, breakeven $94).
- **Building blocks**: risk graphs for long/short stock, calls and puts.
- **Strategies**, grouped the way the course does:
  - Option + stock: protective put, covered call
  - Calls *and* puts: straddle, strangle
  - Calls *or* puts: bull spread, bear spread, butterfly spread
  
  Each has a live profit/payoff chart, legs priced with Black-Scholes, max gain/loss, breakevens, a symbolic payoff table and when to use it.
- **Pricing**: Black-Scholes calculator with sliders for S₀, K, T, σ, r and dividend yield, intrinsic vs. time value, step-by-step formula, put-call parity, no-arbitrage bounds, Greeks and the six-factor table.
- **Binomial tree**: one-step replicating-portfolio valuation (defaults to the $100 → $120/$90, K = $110 example, $6.19).
- **Practice**: worked examples (Genco Oil protective put and covered call, Black-Scholes, parity, early exercise, arbitrage, margin) and the limits of Black-Scholes.

Concepts follow Bodie, Kane & Marcus, *Essentials of Investments*. Educational only; not investment advice.
